All assets used in Dragon Escape are free ,non-AI pixel art created by indie artists on itch.io.
All sound effects and music are synthesized in code using Web audio API.
##Assets
-Cave background,back walls and tiles - https://ansimuz.itch.io/caverns-environment
-Dragon - https://deepdivegamestudio.itch.io/dragon-asset-pack
-Fireball and golden orb - https://bdragon1727.itch.io/fire-pixel-bullet-16x16
-Spikes - https://bdragon1727.itch.io/free-trap-platformer
-Rocks - https://larchrow.itch.io/larchrow-16x16-effects-projectiles/devlog/1746601/31-animated-16x16-effects-and-projectiles-drawn-by-code-free
-Player ball - https://axiale.itch.io/animated-orb-assets
-Buttons - https://greatdocbrown.itch.io/gamepad-ui
